About Our Events

The Levant Strategic Centre hosts platforms for sustained, region-led conversations on security and strategy rooted in local realities. Our events bring together policymakers, scholars, practitioners, and civil society leaders to address the region's evolving challenges.

Drawing inspiration from global platforms like the Munich Security Conference and the Manama Dialogue, we offer a space in the Middle East, about the Middle East, for the Middle East—grounded in regional priorities and informed by local voices.
